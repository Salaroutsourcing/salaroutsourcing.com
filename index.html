Document Checklists

Know exactly what you need

Standard document requirements for each visa type. Your consultant will give you a tailored list based on your specific country and case.

Personal Documents

Valid passport (min. 2 years validity)
CNIC / B-Form copy
Passport-size photographs (ICAO standard)
Birth certificate
Police clearance certificate
Medical certificate (if required)

Academic Documents

All academic transcripts (HEC attested)
Degree / Certificates
University acceptance letter
IELTS / English test (if required)
Statement of Purpose (SOP)
Gap explanation letter (if applicable)

Financial Documents

6 months bank statements
Sponsor affidavit / bank statements
Proof of tuition payment / scholarship
Accommodation proof
Travel insurance
Visa fee payment proof
⚠️ Requirements vary by country. Germany may require blocked account (Sperrkonto); UK requires CAS number. Your SK Immigration consultant will give you a country-specific checklist.

Personal Documents

Valid passport (min. 3 years validity)
CNIC copy
Passport photographs (biometric)
Police clearance certificate (attested)
Medical fitness certificate

Work & Qualification Docs

Employment contract / job offer letter
CV / Resume (European format)
Degree certificates (attested + translated)
Previous work experience letters
Language certificate (A1/B1 German if required)
Qualification recognition (Anerkennung)

Financial & Legal

Bank statements (3 months)
Accommodation letter from employer
Travel insurance (min. €30,000 coverage)
Completed visa application form
Visa fees payment receipt
⚠️ Ausbildung visas have additional requirements. Germany requires credential recognition; France and Italy require specific labour market tests. Ask your consultant for country-specific requirements.

Personal Documents

Valid passport (min. 6 months validity)
CNIC copy
Passport photographs
Previous visa copies (if any)
Cover letter explaining purpose of visit

Travel Documents

Flight booking / itinerary
Hotel / accommodation bookings
Travel insurance (Schengen: min. €30,000)
Detailed day-by-day itinerary
Invitation letter (if visiting family/friends)

Financial Evidence

6 months bank statements
Salary slips (3 months) or business docs
Employment letter (showing leave approval)
Property documents (ties to home country)
Income tax returns (NTN)
⚠️ For UK, USA and Canada visit visas, strong ties to home country (property, family, job) are essential. Financial strength significantly affects approval chances.

FAQ

Answers to the most common questions

Pakistani visa applicants ask these questions every day. Here are honest, clear answers.

Yes — several European countries regularly accept students with lower grades. Hungary, Poland, Czech Republic, Malta, Cyprus and Romania are popular options for students with 50–65% marks. The key is finding the right university and having strong supporting documents. We assess your full profile — not just your marks — and find the best fit.
Not always. Many European universities — especially in Hungary, Poland, Czech Republic and parts of Germany — offer English-taught programs that don't require IELTS if you studied in English medium in Pakistan. Some universities accept a language interview or internal English test instead. We find IELTS-free pathways based on your specific background.
It depends on the country. Hungary and Poland are among the most affordable — tuition can be €2,000–€6,000/year and living costs €400–€600/month. Germany has no tuition fees at public universities (only a small semester contribution of €150–€350), but living costs around €800–€1,000/month. UK and Australia are more expensive — £15,000–£25,000/year tuition. We'll give you a full cost breakdown during your free consultation.
Total timeline from starting with us to departure is typically 3–6 months. This includes university application (1–2 months), acceptance (2–6 weeks), document preparation (2–4 weeks), and visa processing (4–12 weeks depending on country). We help you plan backwards from your intended start date.
Ausbildung is Germany's prestigious vocational training (apprenticeship) system. You work and train at a German company simultaneously, receiving a monthly salary of €600–€1,000 during training. After 2–3 years you receive a recognized German qualification and can apply for permanent residency. Yes, Pakistanis can apply — but you need basic German language (A1–B1) and to find a willing employer. We have a dedicated Ausbildung jobs page with open positions.
Yes, most European student visas allow part-time work. Germany: 120 full days / 240 half days per year. Hungary: 24 hours/week during term. Poland: no restrictions on hours for international students. UK: 20 hours/week during term, full-time during holidays. Part-time income can help cover living expenses significantly.
Visa decisions are made by embassies and consulates — SK Immigration cannot guarantee any visa approval, and we are honest about this from day one. Our service fee covers preparation and filing support, not visa guarantee. Embassy fees are non-refundable. In the event of a refusal, we analyze the rejection letter and advise on reapplication options or alternative pathways. We discuss all risks openly before you commit.
It depends on the country and your financial strength. Germany allows dependents (spouse/children) on a student family reunion visa if you can show sufficient funds. UK allows family visas after the main applicant is settled. Hungary and Poland have limited dependent visa options for students. We assess your specific situation and advise you on the best approach.
Yes — many European universities accept students with study gaps as long as the gap is explained clearly in the SOP (Statement of Purpose). We specialize in writing compelling gap explanations that show how the candidate used their time productively. Hungary, Poland and Czech Republic are particularly gap-friendly. Gaps above 5 years may need stronger financial and personal justification.
